# Circa customer submission guidance

Circa accepts supported electronic items at eligible collection centres. Browse the map to choose a centre and check its current opening hours and accepted item types. A centre being shown on the map does not promise it is open or accepts every device.

## Location and arrival
A fresh location check must place you within 100 metres of the selected centre before photo capture and final submission. Previously granted location permission is used automatically. You can browse without location access. If access is blocked, enable it in browser or device settings and retry. Poor accuracy requires a fresh fix; map selection alone cannot prove arrival. Your saved item details remain available while travelling or retrying.

## Photo, identification and correction
Take or upload one clear photo showing the whole electronic item. Images are private submission evidence for authorized reviewers. Recognition is advisory. If the photo is blurry, shows unrelated items or cannot identify supported e-waste, retake it or use essential manual details. Recognition cannot establish working condition, exact weight, internal composition or safe handling. The original image remains attached. Edit details corrects the same draft and refreshes acceptance and estimates. A question does not submit the item. Final submission always requires the Confirm and submit control.

## Handling and unknown details
Small, Medium and Heavy are handling classes from an approved taxonomy policy. When no policy applies the class is Unknown; do not infer it from a guessed weight. Observed material hints and inferred components are labelled separately. Do not put damaged, leaking, smoking or swollen batteries into a general bin. Ask centre staff for current handling instructions. Circa cannot guarantee item safety from a photo.

## Confirmation and review
Review the item, photo, quantity, chosen centre and estimated benefits, then explicitly confirm. Only a successful server acknowledgement means the submission was received. If the result is uncertain, check saved status before retrying. Follow the instruction returned for that centre; the application does not invent a bin or record physical receipt from a button click. Authorized staff verify independently and approve or reject. Rejection includes a reviewer comment. Status and comments remain in My Account. Outcome delivery uses the original submission channel when configured; a delayed notification does not undo a review decision.

## Benefits and ownership
Displayed carbon benefit is an estimate with its stated unit and basis. This local sample uses an illustrative provider and does not issue certified carbon credits. Rewards are added only after approval and successful settlement. Submission, review approval, physical receipt and actual recycling are separate events. Selling or gifting an asset transfers its attached carbon with ownership; original approval rewards remain with the contributor. No fixed value or timeline is promised here.

## Accounts, privacy and recovery
Use secure Profile sign-in or registration. Linking Telegram requires a verified launch and proof of the existing customer account; a matching name or email is not account proof. Web and Telegram use the same customer records. Never send a password in chat. Privacy retention and deletion terms require an applicable published policy or support answer; this guidance does not invent a retention period. If AI help is unavailable, use the normal journey controls. Your photo and saved draft remain available.
